Privacy Policy

Updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains what information is stored when you use Mila, why, who sees it and what your rights are, under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and Amendment 13 to that law.

Who is responsible for the information

Marina Kaplan, the creator of the app. Contact by email: a.gen.c2026@gmail.com

What information is stored

  • From your Google account: your name and email address, for sign-in and identification only. There is no access to your contacts, photos or any other information in the account.
  • What you enter: your display name, the challenges you chose, your daily check-ins, your groups, kind words and reactions.
  • Settings: time zone, reminder time and notification preferences.
  • Age: if you asked to enter a category meant for ages 18 and over, you are asked for your date of birth. The date itself is not stored. Only whether the category is open to you, or the date on which it will open, is stored.
  • Notifications: if you turned on notifications, a technical address of your device is stored so that reminders can be sent to it.
  • Anonymous counter: a count of visits and taps that does not identify you, to understand whether the app is working. You can turn it off by tapping “Essential only”.

You are not required by law to provide this information. Without a name and an email address, an account cannot be created.

What the information is used for

  • To run the game: challenges, check-ins, groups and ranking.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you asked for.
  • To keep the app safe and handle reports of abusive behavior.
  • To improve the app, based on aggregate data only.

Who sees what

Friends in a group see your display name, the challenges you opened in that group and your check-ins for them. A challenge you set to “Only me” stays private. Your email address is not shown to anyone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell information and we don't pass it to anyone for marketing purposes.
  • There are no ads and no cross-site tracking.

Where the information is stored

The information is stored with Cloudflare, a cloud infrastructure provider, on servers that may be outside Israel, in countries that maintain adequate protection for information. The transfer is made only for the purpose of operating the service. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS), and access to the information is restricted.

For how long

The information is kept for as long as the account is active. After the account is deleted, the identifying details are deleted, and the history in groups is kept without identification.

Your rights

  • To view your information: in Settings → “Export my data” you get a file with all the information.
  • To correct it: you can change your display name and settings at any time. For any other correction, you can contact us.
  • To delete it: in Settings → “Delete account”.
  • To stop notifications and the counter: in Settings or in the cookie notice.

Cookies

There is one cookie that keeps you signed in (essential). In addition, an anonymous identifier for the counter and preferences such as accessibility are stored on your device. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party cookies.

Age

Use is permitted from age 13. Anyone under 18 uses Mila with the approval of a parent or guardian. A parent who wants to view or delete their child's information can contact us at the address above.

Changes

If the policy changes, we will update it here and note the date of the update.
