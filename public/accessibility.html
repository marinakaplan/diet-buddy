Accessibility statement

Updated: November 11, 2026

It matters to us that everyone can use Mila comfortably, including people with disabilities. We have worked to adapt the app to the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and to WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we did

  • The app is built in English, left to right, and is declared that way for screen readers too.
  • Color contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text.
  • You can navigate with a keyboard. There is a clear highlight on the selected element and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Icon-only buttons have a text description for screen readers. Windows also close with the Escape key.
  • Buttons and tap areas are a comfortable size for touch.
  • Text grows with your phone's text size settings. There is no automatic zoom that shifts the page.
  • Animations are turned off when “Reduce Motion” is on in your phone.

The accessibility menu

Every screen has a blue accessibility button. It lets you:

  • Increase text size (three levels)
  • High contrast
  • Highlight links and buttons
  • Stop animations
  • Readable font
  • Reset the settings

The settings are saved on your device only.

Known limitations

  • Sign-in goes through a Google screen, which is not under our control.
  • The app is in beta. Some parts may not be fully accessible yet. We'd be glad to hear about it and fix it.

Ran into a problem?

If something isn't accessible to you, we'd be glad to hear about it and fix it as quickly as possible.
